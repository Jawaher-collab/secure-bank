SECUREBANK CUSTOMER POLICIES

ACCOUNT OPENING POLICIES

Customers must be at least 18 years old to open a SecureBank account.
Customers must provide valid identification and required personal information.
SecureBank offers CHECKING and SAVINGS accounts.
Each account must be linked to an active SecureBank customer profile.
Customers are responsible for keeping their contact information up to date.


TRANSFER POLICIES

Customers may transfer funds between their own SecureBank accounts.
Customers may also transfer funds to another SecureBank customer.
The maximum daily transfer limit is $5,000.
A transfer cannot be completed if the account has insufficient funds.
The sending account must be active at the time of the transfer.
Customers must verify the transfer amount and destination account before confirming a transfer.
Completed transfers are recorded in the customer's transaction history.


LOAN POLICIES

Customers must have an active SecureBank account before applying for a loan.
Loan applications are subject to review and approval.
Customers must provide the required financial information when applying for a loan.
Submitting a loan application does not guarantee approval.
Loan status may be PENDING, APPROVED, or REJECTED.
Customers can review the status of their loan requests through SecureBank.


CARD POLICIES

Customers with an active SecureBank account may request a bank card.
A card must be associated with an eligible customer account.
Customers must report lost or stolen cards as soon as possible.
SecureBank may block a card when suspicious activity is detected.
Customers are responsible for protecting their card information.


BENEFICIARY POLICIES

Customers may add beneficiaries for transfers.
A beneficiary must be registered before the customer can initiate certain transfers.
Customers are responsible for verifying beneficiary information.
Customers should not add unknown or untrusted individuals as beneficiaries.


SECURITY POLICIES

Customers must keep their SecureBank login credentials confidential.
Customers must never share their password with another person.
SecureBank employees must not request a customer's password.
Customers should report suspicious account activity as soon as possible.
SecureBank may restrict access when suspicious authentication activity is detected.
Customer access is controlled according to the user's assigned role.
Sensitive banking operations require an authenticated customer.


TRANSACTION POLICIES

Deposits and withdrawals are recorded in the customer's transaction history.
Transfers are recorded after they are successfully processed.
Customers may view transactions associated with their own accounts.
Customers cannot access another customer's private transaction information.
Transactions must contain sufficient information for SecureBank to maintain an audit trail.
